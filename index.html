From the Founder's Desk

Dear Partners and Investors,

In 2017, an idea took root — not in a boardroom, but in my own life as a father.

Like every parent, I wanted to stay connected to my children's learning journey. Yet I found myself facing the same challenge experienced by millions of families: a communication gap between parents, teachers, students and school administration.

Important information was fragmented. Meaningful collaboration was difficult. Technology existed, but it wasn't truly bringing people together.

That personal experience became the foundation of VRRITTAM.

VRRITTAM was born not from a business opportunity, but from a father's responsibility.

VRRITTAM was never created simply to build another educational application. It was created to bridge relationships, simplify communication and enable every stakeholder in education to work together with clarity and confidence.

Our vision is bold. If a person owns a smartphone, VRRITTAM should become one of the applications they trust every day for education.

Not because it is mandatory. But because it makes education simpler, smarter and more connected.

This is only the beginning of our journey.

CR. Sathish Kumar Founder & Managing Director
WinjoSys Technologies Private Limited
